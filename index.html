A responsive frontend attendance management application for KALAIMAGAL COLLEGE OF ARTS AND SCIENCE. It supports account creation, secure login demo flow, attendance capture, history tracking, and management reporting using localStorage.

About Smart Attendance

Smart Attendance is designed to manage student attendance in a simple and organized way. Students can create an account, log in with their username and password, verify their location, capture a live photo, complete biometric verification, and then mark attendance. All attendance records are stored in localStorage for this frontend-only demonstration.

The system is useful for reducing manual work, keeping records organized, and helping management monitor attendance without paper registers. Attendance history is maintained automatically so that each student's present and absent status can be reviewed anytime.
